4. Emergency and time-sensitive matters

NyaySetu is not an emergency or crisis service. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In India, dial 112. Do not wait for NyaySetu if the matter involves arrest, imminent violence, a court appearance, a limitation deadline, evidence preservation or another time-sensitive right.
