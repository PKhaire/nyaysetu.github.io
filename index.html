Immediate danger or urgent legal deadline?

NyaySetu is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services or the appropriate authority immediately. In India, dial 112 for emergency assistance. For an arrest, court date, limitation deadline or immediate safety risk, seek a qualified advocate without waiting for a bot response.

Frequently asked questions

Know what NyaySetu is — and is not

No. NyaySetu is a legal-information and consultation-coordination platform. General automated information is not a substitute for advice from a qualified advocate who has reviewed your complete circumstances.

No payment is required to begin the guided information flow. The ₹499 fee applies only when you choose and confirm an offered consultation booking, unless a different fee is clearly presented before payment.

No. Legal outcomes depend on facts, evidence, procedure, applicable law and decisions made by courts or authorities. Neither information nor consultation can guarantee an outcome.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, complete payment-card data or unnecessary identity documents. Avoid intimate or highly sensitive evidence unless a verified professional specifically requests it through an appropriate channel.

Eligibility depends on the timing and reason. Review our Cancellation Policy and Refund Policy, or contact support before the scheduled consultation.

The automated guided flow can start immediately. Human support and professional consultations depend on working availability and a confirmed slot. Do not wait for NyaySetu in an emergency or where a legal deadline is approaching.

NyaySetu verifies the payment status and sends a booking confirmation using your registered contact details. If payment succeeds but you do not receive confirmation, contact support with the payment reference—never share an OTP, UPI PIN or full card number.

The guided experience supports English, Hindi and Marathi. Availability of a professional in a preferred language is confirmed during booking.
