Before you write

Share a short summary—not your whole case file

A category, current stage and clear question are enough for initial intake. An advocate may request relevant details later through an appropriate channel.

1

Name the issue

Choose the closest category and describe what decision or next step you need help understanding.

2

Mention real urgency

State a known court, police or statutory date. Do not guess. Intake does not calculate or pause deadlines.

3

Protect sensitive information

Do not include OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords, full card data, Aadhaar numbers or unnecessary identity documents.

!

Danger, arrest or a deadline today?

Do not wait for this intake. Contact emergency services, the appropriate authority or a qualified local advocate immediately. In India, call 112 for emergency assistance.
